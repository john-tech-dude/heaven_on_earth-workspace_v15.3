Six Revenue Streams: Community Supported Agriculture (CSA), Community Kitchen, Value-Added Products (fermented vegetables, herbal tinctures), Agritourism & Education, Corporate CSA Programs, and Work-Trade Program.

Presenter Note: This pillar eliminates food costs while generating revenue. The $300K savings is direct DSCR improvement.
