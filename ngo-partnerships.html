01 — Legal Structure

CONTRACTING FRAMEWORK

OECD-Compliant Transfer Pricing

Arm's-Length Partnership Structure: All NGO partnerships utilize market-standard royalty mechanisms with strict liability isolation. No joint ventures. No shared ownership. Clean contractual boundaries.

Core Protections:

  • "No Joint Venture" Clause: Explicitly prevents partnership characterization—only licensor-licensee relationship
  • IP Quarantine: Third parties receive non-transferable, revocable licenses for specific programmatic deployment only
  • OECD Transfer Pricing: 5-8% royalty band (validated by benchmarking studies against comparable educational/spiritual institutions)
  • Liability Firewall: Separate legal entities prevent operational risks from affecting Foundation assets

Audit & Compliance: Annual third-party verification of arm's-length compliance. Full documentation for IRS and lender review.

Presenter Note: The OECD 5-8% royalty band is institutionally validated. Kingship Manna at 6% (midpoint) demonstrates market-standard compensation. The "No Joint Venture" clause is critical—prevents lenders from worrying about hidden partnership liabilities. IP quarantine means Foundation owns all curriculum, branding, and methodology.

04 — Case Study

INSTITUTIONAL NEXUS

Kingship Manna Coalition

Template for All Future IP Relationships: Zero ownership transfer—only limited, revocable license for programmatic deployment. Mandatory U.S. Resident Trustee oversight ensures domestic tax-exempt "Mind and Management" continuity.

Structural Safeguards:

  • Zero Ownership Transfer: All proprietary methodologies remain exclusive Foundation IP in perpetuity
  • Limited Usage Rights: Non-transferable, non-sublicensable, revocable licenses only
  • Quality Control: Foundation maintains standards through certification requirements and annual audits
  • Termination Rights: License revocable if partner fails to meet operational or ethical standards
  • U.S. Resident Oversight: Mandatory participation of U.S. Resident Trustee in all IP decisions

Financial Structure:

  • Base Royalty: 6% of gross revenue from programs utilizing Foundation curriculum
  • Performance Adjustment: Rate escalates to 7.5% if partner exceeds $500K annual revenue
  • Minimum Guarantee: $12,000/year floor ensuring Foundation cost coverage
  • Audit Rights: Foundation retains right to inspect partner books with 30-day notice

Template Status: Kingship Manna structure serves as binding template for all future NGO partnerships. Material deviations require Board resolution.

Presenter Note: Kingship Manna demonstrates the full framework in operation: OECD-compliant pricing, IP quarantine, U.S. Resident oversight, and documented audit rights. The revocable nature protects Foundation—if partner acts against mission or quality standards, license terminates. This is not a joint venture; it's a controlled, monitored, arm's-length relationship.
