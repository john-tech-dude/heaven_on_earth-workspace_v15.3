Corporate Structure

The Crown-Down Architecture

Five-entity institutional firewall isolating philanthropic assets from commercial operations. Absolute bankruptcy remoteness and mission integrity in perpetuity.

Apex Entity

THE CROWN

South Dakota Perpetual Purpose Trust

The Sovereign Layer. The Foundation's ultimate holding vehicle structured as a South Dakota Perpetual Purpose Trust (PPT). South Dakota represents the global gold standard for institutional asset protection.

  • Steward-Ownership Model: Legally separates equitable ownership from management. Locks the mission (the 'Immutable Seed') in perpetuity, strictly prohibiting extraction or sale of core assets.
  • Bankruptcy Remoteness: Absolute, legally fortified firewall between capitalized reserves and any downstream commercial operations or liabilities.
  • Privacy & Tax: Automatic perpetual sealing of court records. Zero state income tax on undistributed capital.
Presenter Note: The irrevocable nature of the PPT is what makes this structure institutionally credible. Unlike typical LLCs that can be pierced, this trust structure is specifically designed to be bankruptcy-remote and is recognized by sophisticated lenders and institutional partners.
Asset Fortress

THE SHIELD

§508(c)(1)(A) IP Custodian

The Intellectual Fortress. A 508(c)(1)(A) religious non-profit holding all trademarks, brand equity, curriculum IP, and blueprints. Intentionally operationally sterile — it owns everything but touches nothing.

  • Asset Protection: Holds all sanctuary IP, curriculum, and methodologies. Zero commercial debt — completely "clean" entity.
  • Tax Designation: Backed by a formal Tax Opinion Letter from a Tier-1 accounting firm (KPMG), ensuring master IP is held in an incontrovertible tax-exempt fortress.
  • Royalty Revenue: 5-8% gross revenue royalty from Engine creates sustainable funding without operational liability.
Key Point: The Shield never signs contracts, never employs staff, never operates commercially. This sterility is what protects the IP from any downstream litigation.
Fiduciary Governance

THE COMPASS

Independent Fiduciary Board

The Governance Guardian. An independent Board of Trustees with legally binding fiduciary duty to protect Foundation assets and mission integrity. This eliminates "Founder Whim" and proves institutional governance.

  • Board Powers: Veto power over all expenditures exceeding $100K, ratification of major contracts, and sole hiring/firing authority over the CFO.
  • Compensation Governance: Founders compensated as employees at board-determined rates. No equity interest, no profit participation. Surplus flows 100% back to mission.
  • Independent Majority: 5-seat board with 3 independent trustees ensures unbiased oversight.
Critical: The Board's power over CFO hiring and $100K+ expenditures is what gives institutional lenders confidence that capital will be stewarded professionally, not whimsically.
Risk Isolation

THE ENGINE

Wyoming LLC (Taxable OpCo)

The Commercial Risk Silo. All retreat operations, employment, and hospitality liability housed in a standalone, taxable Operating Company. Legally isolates the Foundation from commercial exposure.

  • Jurisdictional Defense: Wyoming LLC provides charging order protection (creditors can't seize assets) and zero state corporate income tax.
  • Operational Liability: Signs all employment contracts, assumes all hospitality liability, processes all guest revenue.
  • Institutional Compliance: 5-8% gross revenue royalty to Shield backed by OECD arm's-length standards. Secured by $5M key-person insurance per founder.
Why Wyoming: Charging order protection means even if the Engine is sued, creditors can't seize Foundation assets. The corporate veil is the strongest in the U.S.
Localized Execution

THE ANCHORS

Regional Special Purpose Vehicles (SPV)

The Local Bridge. Jurisdiction-specific SPVs handling regional compliance, zoning, and local regulatory requirements. Each operates as an independent legal silo.

  • British Columbia Society: Phase 1 | Operations — Canadian regulatory compliance and local land use.
  • Thailand BOI Company: Phase 2 | 8-year 0% tax holiday under Board of Investment incentives.
  • Bali PT PMA: Phase 3 | ASEAN corridor access with Indonesian foreign investment status.
  • Portugal TER: Phase 3 | EU entry and European grant eligibility.

Submarine Strategy: Each campus is legally watertight — no cross-collateralization. Localized compliance without affecting the global network.

Expansion Logic: New locations launch as Anchors under Engine's operational umbrella while maintaining local regulatory independence. Failure in one jurisdiction cannot cascade.
