The BC Flagship

Proof-of-concept sanctuary on British Columbia's Sunshine Coast. Asset-light management, 40-bed capacity, staged expansion to Thailand Phase 2.

01 — Location

SUNSHINE COAST, BC

Confirmed Primary Parcel

Strategic Location: 40-minute ferry from Vancouver, accessible yet secluded. British Columbia's Sunshine Coast offers a proven wellness tourism corridor with established infrastructure and year-round temperate climate.

Asset-Light Structure:

  • Management Agreement Model: 10-year renewable lease with performance-based extensions—not a property acquisition
  • Reduced Capital Intensity: Avoids $4–6M land purchase; capital deployed to program infrastructure instead
  • Proven Hospitality Infrastructure: Existing 40-bed facility with operational kitchen, guest rooms, and meeting spaces
  • First-Mover Timeline: 4-6 month operational ramp vs. 18-24 months for ground-up construction

Site Advantages: Waterfront access, 40+ acres of forested land, existing zoning for wellness/retreat use, proximity to Vancouver International Airport (90 min total journey), established ferry service with 10+ daily sailings.

Presenter Note: This is a management agreement, not a real estate acquisition. We avoid tying up capital in land purchase, instead deploying it to program infrastructure. The 10-year lease with renewal options provides operational stability without asset ownership risk. The Sunshine Coast is an established wellness destination—proven market, not speculative location.
02 — Structure

ASSET-LIGHT MODEL

Management Agreement

Why Asset-Light: The Foundation prioritizes capital deployment to programming and people rather than real estate ownership. This model eliminates land acquisition risk while preserving flexibility for future expansion decisions.

Lease Structure:

  • Base Rent: Fixed monthly payment plus percentage of gross revenue above threshold
  • Performance Clauses: Automatic renewal triggers at 60%+ annual occupancy
  • Exit Provisions: 12-month wind-down clause protects operations if relocation needed
  • Improvements: Leasehold improvements (up to $500K) amortized over lease term

Financial Impact:

  • CapEx Avoided: $4–6M land acquisition not required
  • Working Capital: Preserved for program development and operational reserves
  • DSCR Benefit: Lower fixed costs improve debt coverage ratio
Presenter Note: Traditional retreat centers spend 40-60% of capital on land acquisition. Our asset-light model puts that capital to work in programs and operations instead. The management agreement includes performance-based renewal triggers—if we succeed, we stay; if we underperform, we have flexibility to relocate. This is operational agility that property ownership cannot match.
03 — Facility

40-BED CENTER

Wellness Infrastructure

Capacity & Configuration: 40 beds across private and shared accommodation, designed for both intimate retreats (12-16 participants) and larger intensives (30-40 participants).

Facility Components:

  • Dyad Rooms: 8 dedicated dyad practice spaces with sound insulation
  • Contemplation Hall: 2,000 sq ft main hall for group sessions (seats 50)
  • Thermal Suite: Sauna, cold plunge, and steam facilities
  • Healing Center: 4 treatment rooms for clinical somatic work
  • Farm Integration: On-site greenhouse (2,000 sq ft) and 5-acre cultivation
  • Community Kitchen: Commercial kitchen producing 3 meals/day for 60+

Occupancy Model:

  • Break-Even: 35% occupancy (14 beds) — 40-point safety margin vs. industry
  • Target: 65% average annual occupancy (26 beds)
  • Peak: 85% summer capacity (34 beds)
Presenter Note: 40-bed capacity is the sweet spot—large enough for economies of scale, small enough for intimate community. The industry standard requires 60-70% occupancy to break even; our vertical integration (farm food, work-study labor) drops break-even to just 35%. This 40-point safety margin is the difference between surviving a downturn and failing.
04 — Financials

FUNDING GATES

Staged Capital Deployment

Staged Deployment Model: Capital released in tranches tied to verified milestones. This protects lender capital by ensuring proof-of-concept before major commitments.

Gate 1: Foundation ($1.5M)

  • Trigger: Management agreement signed, 6-month operating reserve secured
  • Use: Leasehold improvements, initial staffing, program development
  • Timeline: Months 1-3

Gate 2: Scale ($3.5M)

  • Trigger: 6-month DSCR >1.2x, 40%+ occupancy achieved
  • Use: Farm build-out, healing center equipment, staff housing
  • Timeline: Months 4-12

Gate 3: Optimize ($2-4M)

  • Trigger: 12-month DSCR >1.4x, 60%+ sustained occupancy
  • Use: Expansion to 60 beds, Thailand Phase 2 seed capital
  • Timeline: Year 2

Key Metrics: 1.40x DSCR at 35% occupancy, 4-6% non-compounding return, $1.2M debt service reserve in escrow, 100% principal protection.

Presenter Note: This is staged capital deployment with hard gates. Lender capital is protected—$1.2M sits in escrow for 6 months of debt service. Each gate requires demonstrated performance: Gate 2 needs 1.2x DSCR and 40% occupancy before any additional capital flows. This is not "hope and pray"—this is verified milestones governing capital release.
