CFO Authority:

  • Joint Signing: CFO maintains joint signing authority on reserve accounts with lender representatives
  • Monthly Reporting: Standardized financial reports to Board and lenders within 15 days of month-end
  • Covenant Monitoring: Real-time DSCR tracking with automated alerts at 1.3x threshold

Succession Protocol (3-Stage):

  • Stage 1 (Years 1-3): Founder-led with CFO oversight and board veto authority
  • Stage 2 (Years 4-7): CEO transition to external hire; founders remain on board
  • Stage 3 (Year 8+): Founders move to "Cultural Guardian" ceremonial roles; zero operational authority
Presenter Note: The CFO requirement is a standard institutional safeguard. Joint signing authority means the CFO AND a lender representative must both approve reserve account withdrawals. This prevents founders from accessing capital without oversight. The 3-stage succession plan eliminates "founder risk" that collapses personality-dependent organizations.

Escrow Governance:

  • Release Triggers: Debt service payments auto-release; operational draws require CFO + Board Chair approval
  • Replenishment: Reserve must be maintained at 6-month coverage; quarterly true-up from operating cash flow
  • Lender Visibility: Real-time account access via banking portal; monthly reconciliation reports
  • Failure Protocol: If reserve drops below 3 months, automatic covenant review triggered

Annual Escrow Audit: Independent verification that reserve balance matches recorded liabilities; lender receives certified audit letter.

Presenter Note: The $1.2M reserve is lender protection—it's not for operations, it's for debt service if revenue falls short. Dual signatory means both Foundation CFO AND lender representative must approve any release. This is standard practice in institutional debt; unusual in nonprofit lending.
04 — Capital Deployment

STAGED GATES

Performance-Locked Tranches

Tranche Release Model: Capital deployed in stages tied to verified milestones. Each gate requires demonstrated performance before next tranche releases.

Gate Amount Trigger Timeline
Gate 1 $1.5M Agreement signed Month 1
Gate 2 $3.5M 6-mo DSCR >1.2x Month 6+
Gate 3 $2-4M 12-mo DSCR >1.4x Year 2

Gate Review Process:

  • Quarterly Board Review: Financial performance assessed against gate criteria
  • Lender Notification: 30-day advance notice of gate trigger or failure
  • Gate Failure Protocol: If metrics not met, capital held until performance improves; no penalty, no default
  • Acceleration Option: Lender may elect to accelerate next tranche early if performance exceeds targets
Presenter Note: This is NOT "draw down as needed." Capital is LOCKED until gates open. Gate 2 requires 1.2x DSCR with 6 months of data—real performance, not projections. This protects lender capital by ensuring proof-of-concept before major deployment. If BC Flagship underperforms, remaining capital never leaves escrow.
