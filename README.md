# Heaven on Earth Foundation - Project Overview

## What This Project Is

This is a fundraising website and presentation package for the **Heaven on Earth Foundation**, a non-profit organization that wants to build wellness retreat centers. Think of it as a business plan website designed to attract investors and donors.

## The Big Idea

The foundation wants to create places where people can go to improve their health and wellness through a holistic approach that combines:

1. **Organic Farming** - Growing their own food using sustainable methods
2. **Education** - Teaching people about healthy living
3. **Healing** - Offering health treatments and therapies
4. **Spiritual Growth** - Providing meditation and self-discovery programs

## What They're Asking For

They're seeking **$6-9 million in loans** from impact investors and philanthropic partners to:
- Build their first location in British Columbia, Canada
- Create a 40-bed wellness center
- Set up organic farming operations
- Establish programs and facilities

## Why They Think It Will Work

Their key advantages:
- **Lower costs than competitors** - They break even at 35% occupancy when most similar places need 60-70%
- **Vertical integration** - They grow their own food, run their own programs, and don't outsource much
- **Work-study program** - People can work in exchange for reduced rates, saving ~$200K annually in labor costs
- **Non-profit structure** - All profits go back into the mission, not to shareholders

## The Website Structure

The project includes several web pages:

- **index.html** - The main prospectus with all the business details, financial projections, and investment information
- **flagship-dashboard.html** - Interactive details about their first planned location in British Columbia
- **ecosystem-dashboard.html** - Explains their four-pillar approach (farm, school, healing, enlightenment)
- **ngo-partnerships.html** - How they plan to work with other organizations
- **board-oversight.html** - Their governance and financial controls
- **Additional presentation files** - Other formats for presenting the information

## Who It's For

This website is designed for two main audiences:
1. **Impact Lenders** - Investors who want to make money while supporting a good cause
2. **Philanthropic Partners** - Donors who want to support the mission

## The Investment Pitch

They're offering:
- 4-6% return on investment (non-compounding)
- 100% principal protection
- Asset-backed security
- Strong financial safeguards including a $1.2M reserve fund

## Technical Details

The website is built as:
- Static HTML pages with premium styling
- Interactive dashboards with expandable cards
- Professional presentation format
- Designed for both web viewing and PDF export
- Responsive design for different screen sizes

## Summary

In simple terms, this is a professional fundraising package for a wellness non-profit that wants to build retreat centers where people can improve their health through organic food, education, healing treatments, and spiritual programs. They're asking for loans to build their first location and have structured it as an attractive investment opportunity with both financial returns and social impact.
